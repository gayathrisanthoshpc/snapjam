FAQ

Questions
answered.

Everything you need to know about snapjam.

Is snapjam really free? ↓
Yes, completely free. No hidden plans, no credit card required, no limits on how many photos you can analyze. We built this as a tool we'd want to use ourselves.
Are my photos saved anywhere? ↓
No. Your photo is sent directly to Claude AI to analyze, and immediately discarded. We don't store, log, or share your images. Privacy is built into how the app works, not added as an afterthought.
Which languages do the songs come from? ↓
snapjam matches songs from 50+ languages including Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Korean, Spanish, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Turkish, Swahili, and many more. The AI picks languages based on what fits your photo's vibe — not randomly.
Will the songs actually be on Instagram? ↓
We specifically prompt the AI to only suggest songs available on Instagram's music library. Most trending songs are there. If you can't find one, try searching just the song title without the artist name.
What types of photos work best? ↓
All kinds! Selfies, landscapes, food shots, travel photos, aesthetic flats, candid moments — the AI reads context from any image. Photos with clear mood or setting tend to give the most accurate matches.
Can I use the mood selector and upload a photo together? ↓
Yes! The mood selector is optional but adds extra context. If you select "Romantic" and upload a sunset photo, the AI considers both signals together for a more refined match.
How does the AI actually work? ↓
snapjam uses Claude AI's vision model, which can "see" and understand images. It reads the emotional atmosphere of your photo — the colors, lighting, subject, setting — and maps that to musical qualities like tempo, energy, and genre.
Can I share my results? ↓
Yes! After you get your 3 songs, there's a "Share your match" button that creates a shareable summary of your vibe tags and song matches. You can copy the text or use your phone's native share sheet.

We know that feeling — perfect photo, wrong song. Scrolling Instagram's music library for minutes, nothing fits. snapjam fixes that with AI.
